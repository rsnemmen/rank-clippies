    Methodology

    On each benchmark, a model's score is its gap to the best tracked model as a share of the benchmark's range: (best − score) / (best − floor), so 0 = best and 1 = the floor. A model's median gap across its n benchmarks is then blended with a below-average result (the 75th percentile of all models' medians) counted as one extra benchmark: (n × median + prior) / (n + 1). Thin evidence is pulled harder: one benchmark keeps 50% weight on its own result, three keep 75%, ten keep 91%. Uncertainty comes from resampling the category's benchmarks with replacement 2,000 times and re-ranking each time: the tooltip's rank range covers the middle 68% of those ranks. Tiers are contiguous in rank: a model joins the tier leader while it matches or beats the leader in at least 16% of resamples. Error bars span Q1 to Q3 of a model's per-benchmark gaps, blended the same way; they show spread across benchmarks, not uncertainty in the median (none shown for n<3). Marker size in the ranking chart scales with cost. Diamonds = open-weight models. † = n=2 benchmarks    ‡ = n=1 benchmark.
